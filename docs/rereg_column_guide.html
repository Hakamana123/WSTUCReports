What each column of the re-registration report means

One reference so coaches, team leads and the data team read the report the same way. Column letters are for the Coach View tab, which is also the layout of every per-coach file in the zip download.

The full sheet (Query1) has the raw extract first and the same advice columns after it, in AK–AU.

Grey shows up in three situations

Mid-semester report

When the target ends in Block 3, Blocks 1–2 (T, U) have already run. Those cells show what the student was enrolled in, with its result:

  • GEDU1001 ✓ passed
  • GEDU1001 ✗ failed (F, FNS, E or W)
  • GEDU1001 enrolled, no grade recorded yet
  • 0 not enrolled in that block at all

Only Blocks 3–4 (V, W) are advice. Anything still owed from Blocks 1–2 is in the Advice Reason, e.g. “still owes X, Y (take next Autumn)”.

Paused students

Deferred or Leave of Absence (column I). Every advice cell is grey, because the plan is provisional until they confirm they're returning. Here grey does not mean a past registration.

Summer report

A grey subject is one the student still needs that doesn't run in Summer at their campus, or the Summer block is full. They take it in a later session, and the Advice Reason says which.

The ✓ / ✗ / 0 marks exist only on the mid-semester report, and only for this session's Blocks 1–2. That's the only place the file carries a result for each block. Everywhere else a failed subject and a never-attempted one look the same in the data: both are just “still to pass”.

Column by column

A
STUDENT_ID

Student number.

B–D
FIRST_NAME, LAST_NAME, PREFERRED_NAME

Name. Use the preferred name in comms.

E
INSTITUTION_EMAIL_ADDRESS

Student email.

F
Coach

Assigned success coach. It also decides which per-coach file the student lands in.

G
PROGRAM_CD

Diploma program code, e.g. 7196. 9031 is Nursing.

H
COMMENCEMENT_PERIOD

When they started. Decides current vs old cohort, and whether they count as commencing.

I
STUDY_PATH_STATUS

Active Study Path, or Deferred / Leave of Absence, which means paused.

J
Progression Outcome

Raw standing from the extract: Good Standing, At Risk, Conditional Enrolment, Exclusion. Blank for some students; column K explains the blanks.

K
Study Status

Column J with no silent blanks: the outcome if there is one, otherwise Not yet assessed (commencing), Not assessed (paused) or No outcome recorded - review for a student who should have an outcome and doesn't.

L
Other Enrolment

Also enrolled: program X (coach) when the same student has a second program row. Check which one is real.

M
Student Status

Count of what's left, e.g. “Outstanding: 1 prep, 2 core, 2 electives”, or “All passed”.

N
Progress Bar

The same count as a bar and percentage.

O
Progress

Position by position across the whole course: ✓ passed, ◐ registered now (not passed yet), ○ still to pass.

○ covers failed and not yet attempted, because the file can't tell them apart. That's why it isn't ✗, which means “failed” in T and U.

P
Withdrawal Flag

Mid-semester only, for commencing students with no pass in Blocks 1–2 this session. See the flags below. Blank for everyone else, and always blank for a paused student, who gets a note in the Advice Reason instead.

Q
Messaging Template

Which email and tab: Commencing, Template 1A / 1B / 1C / 2 / 3, Transition. Paused students are also collected on the Paused – check enrolment tab of the per-coach files.

R
Rereg Principle

The situation behind the template: On Pattern, Mostly Progressing, 3+ Sessions, and so on.

S
Prep Registration Advice

Prep subject to register (GEDU0016 or GEDU0017), if any.

T
Block 1 Registration Advice

Block 1 advice. On a mid-semester report: what they took in Block 1 and its result, in grey.

U
Block 2 Registration Advice

Block 2 advice. On a mid-semester report: what they took in Block 2 and its result, in grey.

V
Block 3 Registration Advice

Block 3 subject to register.

W
Block 4 Registration Advice

Block 4 subject to register.

X
Earliest Completion

Soonest they could finish at full load. (est.) marks a projection; blank when it can't be estimated.

Y
Advice Reason

The plain-English why: what to register, what's owed later, the Conditional Enrolment cap, paused notes, and assumptions to sanity-check. Read this before contacting the student.

In the per-coach files, S–W also carry the subject name after each code, e.g. GEDU1001 — Name.

Withdrawal Flag (column P)

Only on a mid-semester report, and only for a commencing, not paused student with no pass in Blocks 1–2 this session. The Advice Reason starts with a line saying which case applies.

ADVISE WITHDRAWAL
When
At least one ✗ in Blocks 1–2 (the other block can be ✗ or 0), and registered in Block 3 or 4.
What to do
Withdrawal conversation: drop Blocks 3–4 and restart next semester. No subject advice is given. The Advice Reason lists the failed subjects.
CHECK ENROLMENT
When
Not enrolled in Block 1 or 2 (0 and 0), or no Block 3/4 registration, so there's nothing to withdraw from.
What to do
Not a withdrawal. Confirm the enrolment is right, or whether they're still studying. Normal advice is shown.
AWAITING GRADE
When
Enrolled in Block 1 or 2 with no grade recorded yet.
What to do
Nothing yet. Re-run the report once grades are in. Normal advice is shown.

Rules worth knowing

  • Conditional Enrolment is capped at 30 credit points per semester. The cap includes anything already taken in Blocks 1–2 this session. Inside the cap the order is failed or owed subjects first, then electives, then prep. Whatever doesn't fit is listed under “Still to pass (later session)”.
  • Exclusion: no advice. Refer to the coach.
  • Paused: advice is provisional (grey), no withdrawal advice, and no comms until they confirm they're returning.
  • Full sheet only (Query1): Advice Source says which engine produced the row (Grant calculator, position-based, v2 rule-tree, withdrawal, Summer). It's for checking, not for coaches.
